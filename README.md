## Key Implemented Features

1. **Trilingual & Full RTL Engine**:
   - Afaan Oromoo (Primary / Default)
   - English (Secondary)
   - Arabic (Secondary with full bidirectional RTL layout support)
   - Site-wide manual language switcher: `Oromoo | English | العربية`
2. **14 Ordered Homepage Sections**:
   - Hero Slider (Founder Misbah Sheikh Husein on load &rarr; 4-second cycling of 4 studio slides)
   - Breaking News Ticker (below header, toggled ON/OFF in Admin)
   - Lead News Block with featured story & latest news feed
   - Curated Featured News
   - Dedicated Category Sections (National, Muslim World, World)
   - Barnoota Islaamaa (Qur'aan, Hadiisa, Fiqh, Aqeedah, Tafsir sub-blocks)
   - Seenaa Ulamaa'otaa (Stars of Islam scholar directory)
   - Aalam Podcast Studio Showcase with Listen/Watch buttons
   - Video Library (direct upload + YouTube/TikTok embeds)
   - Photo Gallery (photos only, categorized by album/event with lightbox)
   - Trending / Most Read ranking
   - Newsletter signup box
   - "Become a Member" / "Maatii Aalam Media Ta'i" CTA banner
   - "Support Aalam Media" CTA banner
3. **Interactive Article Detail Page**:
   - Complete metadata, author bylines, timestamps (published & last updated)
   - Social share bar (Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Copy Link)
   - **Correction System**: Visible *"Report an Error / Sirreeffama Gabaasaa"* modal directly submitting to admin
   - **Engagement System**: Reader Reactions (👍 Like, ❤️ Love, 💡 Insightful, 👏 Respect) + Comments thread
4. **Dedicated Media Hubs**:
   - `/podcast`: Episode player with guest/host notes and archive
   - `/video`: Latest Videos, Islamic Videos, Interviews, Short Videos
   - `/audio`: Distinct Audio Library (News audio, Islamic lectures, recitation) with player
   - `/gallery`: Album-based photo gallery with responsive lightbox
   - `/hegere`: Travel chronicles of founder Misbah Sheikh Husein
   - `/ulamaa`: Stars of Islam biographies & contributions
   - `/education`: Comprehensive Islamic educational modules
5. **Community, Member & Support System**:
   - `/member`: Registration form with admin-editable membership tiers (Regular, Volunteer, Contributor, Supporting, Professional)
   - `/support`: Explaining funding needs, strictly displaying the verified *"Payment details coming soon"* placeholder banner with no invented bank details
6. **Newsroom Admin Panel (`/admin`)**:
   - Role switcher simulating granular permissions (Super Admin, CEO/Owner, Editor-in-Chief, Editor, Reporter, Media, etc.)
   - Editorial workflow: Reporter &rarr; Draft &rarr; Editor Review &rarr; Correction &rarr; Publish
   - Breaking News Ticker toggle & headline manager
   - Member management with search, status toggles, and CSV export
   - Advertisement manager (Homepage, Article, Category, Mobile, Desktop) with "Sponsored" distinction
   - Error Reports & Editorial Corrections inbox
   - Newsletter subscribers & CSV export
   - Direct server media upload manager
7. **Persistent Imako Solution Promotion Block** (Section 26):
   - Distinct slim dark footer credit styled site-wide on every page.
